# The Greying prop kit

Low-poly props for the ash land at the edge of the living world: grey dead trees, a dry ash shrub, cracked and smooth stones, two dormant tech relics, and a half-buried seed capsule with one brave flower. 1 unit = 1 m, Y-up glTF, origin at the base centre. Glowing parts (dim seams, starsalt, capsule, flower) are faintly emissive so they still read under overcast light.

Material names: `GR_Wood`, `GR_WoodDark`, `GR_Ash`, `GR_Earth`, `GR_Stone`, `GR_StoneDark`, `GR_Shrub`, `GR_ShrubDark`, `GR_Metal`, `GR_MetalDark`, `GR_Seam`, `GR_Starsalt`, `GR_Capsule`, `GR_Flower`, `GR_Stem`.

## Weaknesses

- The dead trees are branched tubes without bark texture or broken ends that really read as snapped wood.
- The ash shrub foliage is still ico mounds, just greyer than a living bush.
- The cracked stone's split is a dark slab rather than a true fracture surface, and the starsalt is a few small crumbs.
- The tech stub is a fairly boxy pylon; the seam is a simple inset plane, not a dense circuit.
- The seed capsule is a small spun pod on its side, not an open split husk.
